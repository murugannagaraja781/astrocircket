2. Information We Collect

A. User-Provided Information

  • Account Details: When you register an account, we may collect your email address and username. Guest access is fully supported without creating an account.
  • Customer Support: Information you submit when contacting support for troubleshooting or inquiries.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device tokens are collected strictly to transmit live match analysis updates and astrology tips when notifications are enabled.
  • Device & Performance Telemetry: Anonymized crash logs and device specifications (OS version, device model) to maintain app stability.

C. Payment Information

All in-app purchases and unlocked insights are handled securely by certified third-party payment gateways (including PhonePe). We do not collect, process, or store credit/debit card numbers, bank details, or UPI credentials.

3. How We Use Information

  • To display real-time KP cricket astrological analysis and match metrics.
  • To send optional push notifications regarding live match updates.
  • To authenticate your account and sync purchases across devices.
  • To address technical queries and maintain app reliability.

4. Third-Party Integrations

  • Google Play Services: App distribution and licensing.
  • Firebase (Google LLC): Cloud messaging (FCM) and crash diagnostics.
  • Google Sign-In: Secure user authentication.
  • PhonePe: Payment processing for digital content.

5. Account & Data Deletion Policy

You have the absolute right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion:

To delete your account and all associated personal data, please send an email to our support team:

Email: support@sbastro.com

Subject Line: Account Deletion Request

Please send the request from your registered email address and include your username.

Deletion Timeline & Procedure:

  • Processing Timeline: Once your request is received and verified, your account and all associated personal data will be permanently deleted from our active servers within 15 to 30 days.
  • Data That Will Be Deleted: Your profile details, email address, username, authentication credentials, push notification (FCM) tokens, and user preferences.
  • Confirmation: Our support team will notify you via email once the account and data deletion process is completed.
