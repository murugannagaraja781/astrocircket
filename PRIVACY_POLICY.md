## 2. Nature of the Application
**AstroCricket Insights** is a sports analytics and astrological insights platform designed for informational, analytical, and entertainment purposes.

## 3. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide to Us
- **Account Registration Data:** When you choose to create an account, we may collect your username and email address. You may also browse the App as a Guest without creating an account.
- **Support Inquiries:** If you contact our support team, we collect your email address and the content of your message to assist you.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
- **Device & Notification Identifiers:** We collect Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens and basic device information (device model, operating system version) solely to deliver match insights, notifications, and important app updates.
- **App Usage Data:** Non-personally identifiable diagnostic and analytical data to monitor app stability, crash reports, and performance.

### C. Financial & Transaction Information
- If you unlock premium match analysis reports, transactions are processed securely through certified third-party payment gateways (such as **PhonePe**).
- **We do NOT collect, process, or store your credit/debit card numbers, bank account details, UPI PINs, or CVV codes.** All payment processing complies with PCI-DSS standards managed directly by the payment provider.

We use the collected information exclusively to:

4. Provide customer support and address technical issues.
